The case

What the complaint alleges

The lawsuit claims Intel made false and misleading statements to investors during the class period.

1

Intel was more exposed to the slowdown in PC sales than it told investors.

2

Intel's partners were holding unsold inventory, which hurt its ability to raise prices.

3

Gains from AI demand were being offset by weakness in the PC market.

4

When the truth came out, investors who bought during the class period allegedly lost money.

These are allegations only. No court has ruled on them, and the class has not been certified.

Lead plaintiff

What does it mean to lead the case?

The court picks one or more investors to represent everyone in the class. Courts generally look for the investor with the largest financial loss who can fairly represent other shareholders.

You don't have to be lead plaintiff to share in any recovery.
